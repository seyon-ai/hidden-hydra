Privacy

Short version: we collect almost nothing, and what we do store is mostly ciphertext.

What we store

  • Profile: the username, bio, country, emblem, accent color and optional photo you choose, plus a Firebase anonymous UID. No email, no phone number, no real name required.
  • Messages: encrypted blobs in Realtime Database. Plaintext exists only for bot/AI rooms and pre-encryption legacy data.
  • Presence: online flag and last-seen timestamp while using the app.
  • Moderation log: reports and toxicity strikes (message text of flagged messages only).
  • Images you attach: hosted on imgbb under their policy; profiles/photos likewise.

What we never have

  • Your encryption private keys — generated and kept in your browser only.
  • Readable DM or group message content (end-to-end encrypted).
  • Any government ID, contact list, or advertising identifier. There are no ads and no trackers on this site.

Your control

  • Delete your own messages at any time (trash icon on hover).
  • Leave or delete groups; declined friend requests disappear.
  • Clearing browser data removes your local keys and session entirely.
  • Want everything gone? Stop using the anonymous account — with no email tied to it, it becomes unreachable noise.

Third parties

Firebase (auth, database, hosting edge), Vercel (hosting + serverless functions), Groq (AI replies — receives only what your device sends it), imgbb (image hosting), flagcdn (country flag images). Each sees only the slice described above.
