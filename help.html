Chat commands

  • /ai [question] — ask Hydra AI anything, in any room.
  • /help — list the commands.
  • /report @username — send a report to the moderation log.
  • /rules — the community rules.

Tip: the Hydra AI row at the top of the World tab is your own private assistant room — nobody else can read it.

FAQ

Is my chat really encrypted?
Yes — DMs and group messages are sealed on your device with AES-256-GCM before upload. See the Security page for the full model, including what metadata stays visible.
A room says “Syncing encryption keys”.
Group keys are wrapped by members who already hold them. Wait for someone with the key to open the room, press Retry, or start a new epoch from the banner or group info.
The AI doesn't answer.
The AI runs through a serverless function that needs GROQ_API_KEY set in your Vercel environment variables. If it's missing you'll see a friendly bot message saying so.
Image upload fails.
Same idea: IMGBB_API_KEY must be set on Vercel. Images are compressed on-device first (max 1600px).
I lost my phone / cleared browser data.
Your encryption private key lives in that browser only. Old encrypted history from groups you can re-join will resync keys from members; DMs sent to your old key stay private to the old device — that's what end-to-end means.
Why anonymous auth?
Zero friction and zero personal data. Your identity is the profile you choose, protected by Firestore rules keyed to your anonymous UID.
