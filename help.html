FAQ

Is my chat really encrypted?
Yes — DMs and group messages are sealed on your device with AES-256-GCM before upload. See the Security page for the full model, including what metadata stays visible.
A room says “Syncing encryption keys”.
Group keys are wrapped by members who already hold them. Wait for someone with the key to open the room, press Retry, or start a new epoch from the banner or group info.
The AI doesn't answer.
The AI runs through a serverless function that needs GROQ_API_KEY in your Vercel environment variables. Groq retired llama-3.1-8b-instant on 16 Aug 2026 — this build auto-detects the best live model (gpt-oss-20b / qwen3.6-27b / gpt-oss-120b), so an old model id can no longer break it. The bot message now quotes the exact server error.
Image upload fails.
Same idea: IMGBB_API_KEY must be set on Vercel. Images are compressed on-device first (max 1600px).
AI / uploads still broken after adding keys on Vercel?
Run the checklist in order: 1) Open https://your-app.vercel.app/api/health — it shows whether the server can see each key (true/false) without leaking it.
2) If false: env vars only apply to new deployments — after adding them, go to Deployments and Redeploy. Also make sure you added them to the right environment (Production / Preview / Development checkboxes).
3) Names must be exact: GROQ_API_KEY, IMGBB_API_KEY (aliases GROQ_KEY / IMGBB_KEY also work). No spaces or quotes around the value.
4) If health says true but calls fail: your key may be invalid or revoked — rotate it (the old Groq key was committed to your old public repo, so rotate it anyway).
I hid the left panel and can't bring it back.
A gold slide-back handle sits at the middle of the left screen edge whenever the panel is away (desktop collapsed or mobile closed). Tap it — or the three-line icon in any chat header on mobile.
Why aren't world lounges encrypted?
By design. Public rooms with strangers can't require key ceremonies (epochs, unlocks) from every visitor, and they need server-side moderation. World lounges are public plaintext; your DMs and private groups are the end-to-end encrypted spaces — keys never leave your devices there.
DMs or private groups are locked or say "plaintext".
Both people must open the app once with the v2 build — that publishes each side's encryption key. Until the other side does, a DM gracefully falls back to plaintext (with a notice). Once they've been online, press Retry key on the locked notice and everything decrypts. In private groups, any member opening the room shares keys automatically.
After a deploy, some device still shows old bugs / old logo.
Tabs that were already open keep the old code in memory until reloaded, and browsers cache images for days. On every device: close the tab completely and open the site again (or Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+R). Asset URLs carry a version tag now, so a fresh load always pulls the new logo and code.
I lost my phone / cleared browser data.
Your encryption private key lives in that browser only. Old encrypted history from groups you can re-join will resync keys from members; DMs sent to your old key stay private to the old device — that's what end-to-end means.
Why anonymous auth?
Zero friction and zero personal data. Your identity is the profile you choose, protected by Firestore rules keyed to your anonymous UID.
